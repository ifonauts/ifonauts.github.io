IFONAUTS — Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026.

Who we are

Ifonauts is a YouTube channel (youtube.com/@Ifonauts). This policy covers this website and the application ifonauts-factory, which the channel owner uses to manage the channel.

What the application does

ifonauts-factory is used only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's own YouTube account. Through the YouTube API Services it uploads the channel's videos, thumbnails and caption tracks, sets their titles and descriptions, reads the status of the channel's own videos, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports to learn which videos work best. It does not access any other person's YouTube account or data.

The application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is stored, and where

The authorization Google grants (an OAuth refresh token) and the channel's analytics reports are stored on the channel owner's own computer, outside the application's source code, and are never written to logs. Access tokens are short-lived and renewed automatically. No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising.

Viewers and visitors

We do not collect personal data from viewers through the application. YouTube's own analytics reports given to channel owners are aggregated and anonymous. This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections; the application then stops working until it is authorized again. To ask about or request deletion of stored data, contact us at the address below.
